Deletion and retention information

The reviewed mobile code does not implement automatic server-record expiry or scheduled account deletion, and it does not establish a fixed cloud retention period. Records may remain stored after logout. The team's retention periods, deletion-response time, backup handling, and any justified exceptions have not yet been finalized for this policy; deployed retention settings also require review.

To request account deletion, email quadlabs2026@gmail.com with the subject 'Bantay Ulang account deletion' and identify your registered account email. Send the request from your registered email where possible. The team must verify ownership before handling the request; do not send your password. You can also contact this address about access, correction, or privacy questions.

Signing out clears the app's saved approval and signs out the account, but does not erase cloud records or all cached records and notification preferences on the device. The seven-day offline-access limit is an access limit, not a guarantee that cached data is erased after seven days. Clearing app storage removes local app data without deleting server records. Firebase's own service retention and backup removal are separate from the team's retention decisions.
